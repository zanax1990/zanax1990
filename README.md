# Jahanbakhsh Ghasemi

PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Connecticut, with a background in Electrical Engineering. I work at the intersection of machine learning, computational modeling, and deformable systems.

## Research focus

- Kinematics and geometric modeling of soft and shape-changing systems
- Machine learning for physical and biomedical data
- Scientific computing, numerical analysis, and reproducible research workflows
- Data-driven modeling of deformation and time-varying behavior

## Current direction

My current research examines how to quantify and model shape deformation in soft robotic systems. I am interested in combining analytical kinematics with data-driven methods that learn from images, experiments, and simulations.

## Selected software

- [Jahan BioOmics](https://github.com/zanax1990/Jahan-BioOmics) — interactive analysis of label-free DIA proteomics data, including quality control, imputation, PCA, statistical comparison, and pathway enrichment.
- [DIA proteomics preprocessing in R](https://github.com/zanax1990/R) — a staged workflow for filtering, log2 transformation, and per-sample median normalization.
- [Segmentation comparison panels](https://github.com/zanax1990/Ji) — utilities for comparing inputs, ground truth, probabilistic samples, and baseline segmentation outputs.
- [Data visualization projects](https://github.com/zanax1990/data-visualization-projects) — small Python projects for statistical and interactive visualization.

## Technical tools

Python, R, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Plotly, and Streamlit.
